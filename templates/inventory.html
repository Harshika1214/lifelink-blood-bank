UNDERSTANDING STOCK LEVELS

What do the inventory statuses mean?

LifeLink uses the minimum stock level configured for each blood group to indicate whether the available supply requires attention.

Available

Stock is above the configured minimum level.

Low Stock

Units have reached or fallen below the minimum level.

Critical

No units are currently available.
